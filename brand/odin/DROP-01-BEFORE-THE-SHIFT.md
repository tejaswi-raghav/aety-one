# DROP 01 — BEFORE THE SHIFT

## Campaign Premise

Before Odin knows what he is, the world already reacts to him.

DROP 01 follows a recovery job across international transit spaces. Odin has retrieved a sealed artifact from a private archive. The client wants it delivered unopened. His watch has stopped. The case is warm. In every photograph, his shadow points toward somewhere else.

The campaign sells a wardrobe first and opens a mythology second.

## Visual Direction

**Mood:** Expensive restraint, controlled danger, global movement, analogue evidence.

**Locations:** International cargo terminal, archival loading bay, brutalist hotel corridor, night train, private museum storage, coastal runway.

**Palette:** Carbon, oxidized black, bone, tobacco leather, cold concrete, sodium amber.

**Camera:** Editorial realism, 35 mm texture, imperfect available light, shallow depth where useful. No superhero poses and no visible fantasy spectacle.

**Anomaly language:** A shadow at the wrong angle, dust suspended too long, fabric moving in still air, a reflection arriving a fraction late, duplicated architecture at the edge of frame.

## Hero Look

- Charcoal weatherproof technical coat or shell with architectural volume
- Bone or washed-ivory base layer
- Relaxed black tailored utility trousers
- Aged black expedition boots
- Restrained leather and metal accessories
- Camera, stopped watch, pendant, and compact archival case

This remains **canonical concept styling** until real DROP 01 photographs or clean product cutouts are supplied. Generated campaign images must not be presented as accurate product photography before that handoff.

## Shot List

1. **The Retrieval** — full-body hero portrait in an archival cargo bay, case in hand.
2. **Nothing to Declare** — airport customs corridor; calm face, stopped watch visible.
3. **The Case** — close detail of hand, rings, hardware, and unexplained condensation.
4. **Wrong Direction** — wide exterior; Odin and his shadow disagree.
5. **Three Borders** — movement study through a night terminal.
6. **Room 7200** — quiet hotel portrait with a second era faintly reflected in glass.
7. **The Glyph** — garment construction detail paired with the recurring Æ mark.
8. **Before the Shift** — final still: coat caught by impossible wind, expression newly alert.

## Story Rules

- Do not explain the power in DROP 01.
- Never call him a god in launch copy.
- Let humor and competence precede destiny.
- The garment remains legible in every frame.
- One anomaly per image is stronger than a collage of effects.

## Key Line

**Some journeys begin before you know you have left.**
