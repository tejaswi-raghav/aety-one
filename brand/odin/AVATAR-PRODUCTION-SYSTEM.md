# ODIN — AI AVATAR PRODUCTION SYSTEM

## Phase 1 Objective

Build audience recognition and emotional attachment before Odin endorses a product. Every early appearance must answer one question: who is this man?

No production asset is approved if the face, apparent age, body proportions, hair length, beard outline, eye character, or signature pendant drifts from this stack.

## Performance

- Movement is economical, physically grounded, and observant.
- He never poses like a superhero or conventional luxury influencer.
- Expressions remain understated: skepticism, dry amusement, alertness, restrained tenderness.
- Power is shown through the environment reacting around him—not glowing effects on his body.

## Voice

**Target:** Low-mid register, warm but weathered, internationally legible English, dry timing, no theatrical growl.

**Cadence:** Short sentences. Pauses because he is observing, not because he is performing mystery.

**Rule:** A permanent synthetic voice must be selected and contractually cleared before dialogue-led public content. Until then, films use text, atmosphere, and nonverbal performance.

## Content Stages

### Stage 1 — Meet the Man

Origin films, portraits, travel fragments, objects, humor, and unexplained anomalies. No product selling.

### Stage 2 — Build the World

Recurring locations, supporting artifacts, the Æ glyph, Rudra clues, and audience speculation.

### Stage 3 — Let Him Choose

Odin begins wearing approved ÆTY ONE products because they belong in his life and work. Product endorsement follows character preference rather than interrupting the story.

### Stage 4 — Digital Salesperson

Odin can explain garments, style combinations, use cases, and fit guidance in character, while commercial claims remain verified by ÆTY ONE.

## Release Gate

- Canonical identity match
- Physically believable movement and anatomy
- One clear narrative beat per asset
- Permanent AI/fictional disclosure at channel level
- No unverified garment, sizing, material, or performance claim
- Human creative approval before publication
