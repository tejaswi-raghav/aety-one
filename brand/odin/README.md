## Reference hierarchy

1. `reference/odin-primary-photoreal-reference.png` — primary facial identity, physique, skin, hair, beard, and proportions.
2. `reference/odin-canonical-turnaround-v1.png` — canonical wardrobe, equipment, front/back/profile silhouette, and casting views.
3. `reference/odin-expression-sheet-v1.png` — approved emotional range without identity drift.
4. `ODIN-CHARACTER-BIBLE.txt` — personality, lore, voice, styling, and narrative rules.

When references disagree, preserve the facial identity and body from the primary photoreal reference, then use the turnaround for wardrobe and equipment.

## Identity lock

- Apparent age: 27.
- Lean functional climber/sprinter physique; never bodybuilder mass.
- Medium olive, sun-exposed skin with natural texture.
- Shoulder-length, messy dark-brown wavy-curly hair.
- Full dark beard with the same outline and density.
- Strong straight nose, angular jaw, heavy expressive eyebrows, hazel-brown eyes.
- Weathered but youthful; minor natural marks, no glamour retouching.
- Movement and expression remain understated, intelligent, alert, and human.

## Canonical 2027 wardrobe

- Weathered oversized charcoal technical hooded jacket.
- Distressed bone/off-white longline T-shirt.
- Relaxed black utility cargo trousers with straps and pockets.
- Worn black technical boots.
- Battered compact black backpack.
- Dark analog watch, restrained bracelets, and the same small pendant.

## Generation rule

Use the primary photoreal reference and the relevant canonical sheet in every identity-sensitive generation. State explicitly that face, age, body proportions, hair length, beard outline, skin tone, and signature accessories must not change. Change one production dimension at a time: scene, pose, garment, expression, or era.

Avoid fantasy-Viking styling, superhero proportions, glossy skin, neon cyberpunk, perfect symmetry, luxury-influencer posing, random symbols, and unapproved wardrobe changes.

## Approved expression range

- Neutral alert
- Restrained dry smirk
- Curious analytical focus
- Fierce survival focus
- Quiet exhaustion and vulnerability
- Rare genuine warmth

The difference between expressions must come from gaze, micro-tension, posture, and subtle grooming disturbance—not facial redesign.
