# Cyrus Candidate Reference Prompt V1

## Use

Canonical-candidate AI character reference sheet for future identity-consistent still and video production.

## Prompt

Create a premium photorealistic casting/reference sheet for a fictional contemporary Persian/Iranian man named Cyrus. This must feel like one real person photographed in one controlled studio session.

Cyrus is approximately 30, around six feet tall, with a lean athletic fighter/runner physique and broad but not massive shoulders. He has a warm olive complexion, angular masculine face, pronounced cheekbones, strong brows, dark-brown observant eyes, defined jaw, thick medium-short dark hair that is slightly wavy and naturally textured with imperfect strands near the forehead, and short carefully maintained dark stubble.

His gaze is calm, intelligent, and quietly certain—never aggressive. His shoulders remain relaxed and his posture loose.

In every full-body view he wears a cream subtly textured open-collar shirt with sleeves rolled to the forearms, black tailored trousers, black leather boots, and an understated dark watch. No visible logos. His clothing looks expensive through fit, fabric, and restraint. The silhouette is halfway between businessman and adventurer.

Use a seamless warm light-gray studio background with soft north-light studio illumination, subtle fill, natural skin detail, and restrained cinematic contrast.

Compose four separated photographic views of exactly the same man: full-body front, full-body three-quarter, head-and-shoulders neutral portrait, and head-and-shoulders slight knowing half-smile. Preserve identical facial geometry, age, hair, stubble, skin tone, and body proportions across every view.

Avoid stereotypes, orientalist styling, ancient Persian costume, crowns, fantasy elements, flashy luxury, corporate ties, logos, alpha-male posing, crossed arms, exaggerated anger, bodybuilder proportions, plastic skin, face drift, hairstyle drift, typography, and watermarks.
