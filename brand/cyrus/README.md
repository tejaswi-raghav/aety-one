# CYRUS — CHARACTER SYSTEM

> Power isn't inherited. It's constructed.

Cyrus is a contemporary Persian outsider who develops into a strategist, operator, and self-made kingmaker. His defining force is not aggression or wealth; it is certainty.

## Central rule

Cyrus does not become powerful and then develop confidence. His unreasonable confidence causes the world to rearrange itself around him.

## Visual lock

- Persian/Iranian man, apparent age 30
- Lean fighter/runner physique, approximately six feet tall
- Angular face, pronounced cheekbones, strong brows, dark-brown eyes, defined jaw
- Thick, medium-short, naturally wavy dark hair
- Carefully maintained short dark stubble
- Relaxed shoulders, loose posture, observant gaze
- Black unstructured suit, open white or cream shirt, black tailored trousers, black leather shoes, dark watch
- Expensive through fit, material, and restraint—not logos

## Separation from Odin

Odin is movement, myth, instinct, and destiny. Cyrus is stillness, systems, incentives, and constructed power.
